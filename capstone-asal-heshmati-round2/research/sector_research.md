# Research foundation and use-case selection

## Sector and client context
Career education and transition support for individual candidates. Client size is unconfirmed. 40,000 community users and $50/month are provided assumptions, not audited revenue. Supplied workshop transcripts demonstrate TechTalk guidance on role choice, CVs, LinkedIn, networking and interviews, and existing premium AI assistance.

## Three possible use cases
| Use case | Value | Feasibility in 30 hours | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence-based CV + two-JD alignment | Editable outputs directly apply workshop guidance | Highest, paste-first and one sequential workflow | P0 |
| LinkedIn copy and transition roadmap | Consistent public positioning and next learning steps | Moderate, content/learning verification adds work | P1 after core |
| Job discovery and application assistant | Reduces fragmented opportunity monitoring | Lower, source permissions, duplicates and stale listings | Future |

## Opportunities and risks
Opportunity: apply proprietary teaching rules to individual evidence, reduce repetitive coaching tasks, add an integrated subscription module. Risk: invented achievements, coaching that overstates evidence, confusing score with hiring probability, vendor data exposure, source licensing and unverified commercial adoption. Detailed risk matrix: roi_risk_assessment.md.

## Research and dataset evidence boundary
The supplied files are instructional materials. No completed original Round 1 public-dataset analysis or market research was provided. The seven synthetic cases test the product. The five commercial scenario charts analyze planning assumptions. Neither is an empirical labor-market dataset or a replacement for the rubric's public sector research requirement.

## Public dataset candidate for Round 1 completion
Eurostat ICT specialists dataset `isoc_sks_itspt` can inform the employment context, with its official metadata and coverage checked before use: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/isoc_sks_itspt/default/table?lang=en . It describes ICT specialist employment, not TechTalk's willingness to pay or individual hiring success. No values were fetched or charts fabricated from this dataset in this turn. If original Round 1 data exists, preserve it and assess relevance to the pivot rather than replacing it automatically.
