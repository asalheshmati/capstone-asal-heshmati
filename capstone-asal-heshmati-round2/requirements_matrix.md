## Checklist details beyond rubric headings
- Use case: at least two measurable outcomes, stakeholders, boundaries, Round 1 evolution.
- POC: workflow export OR annotated screenshots; a 2–5-minute end-to-end recording; reproduce steps.
- MVP: actual core AI calls, invalid-input handling, README, actual source and dependency manifests, secret placeholders.
- LangSmith: at least five case inputs and references, traces on actual app/POC, ≥1 experiment with evaluator, accessible instructor link OR screenshots plus export, failures and limitations.
- ROI: upfront and ongoing costs, revenue and quantified value, 12/36 months, explicit formula, break-even, ≥6 risks with likelihood/impact/mitigation.
- EU AI Act: step-by-step classification, mandatory requirements for the chosen class, conformity summary of 1–2 pages, technical documentation outline.
- GDPR: data-flow map, processing register, legal bases, retention, DPIA for highest-risk processing, rights and third-party transfers.
- Strategy: explicit pilot before rollout, timeline, stakeholders, GTM, KPIs, commercialization.
- Preserve existing Round 1 artifacts. Add new material, do not overwrite the original research or invent staff feedback.
- Stable Campus identifiers from rubric: Round 1 `project-6`; Round 2 `final-project-gh-repo` and `final-project-presentation-demo`.

Audit statuses use PASS / PARTIAL / MISSING / NEEDS VERIFICATION. Do not sum the rows into an earned grade.
